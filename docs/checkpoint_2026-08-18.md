## What Changed And Why

### Gate B is now checked, not trusted (Task 17)

`research/recompute_gate_b.py` rederives every published pair metric from the matched
coordinates. Distances reproduce to 0.001 px. **AOI agreement does not**: on 4 of 27
pairs one matched sample sits just outside an AOI box under `neurogaze-aoi-v3.1.0`
yet was counted as agreeing at capture time. The capture harness is not in this
repository, so the difference cannot be traced to its source. It is published in
`gate_b_summary.json` under `recomputation`, and **0.997118 is the figure to quote**,
not 0.997574. Gate B still passes (criterion is 0.95).

### Both operating points ship, Youden is the default (Task 20)

`model.json` carried only the sensitivity-0.9 threshold, whose specificity is 0.179 —
at 1% prevalence that refers 82% of a queue. `decision.operating_points` now carries
both, and `default_operating_point` is `youden` (0.4985 → sens 0.731 / spec 0.821),
matching what the paper always reported.

`research/export_operating_points.py` backfills the artifacts from the stored OOF
predictions rather than retraining. Retraining would reproduce the same coefficients
but would also overwrite the degradation study's selection metadata in `training.json`.
`validateModel` now rejects an export whose default point is missing or disagrees with
`refer_if_probability_gte`.

### The Gate C simulation stopped borrowing the CNN's numbers (Task 21)

Four arms, each naming its source, generated by `research/prospective_evaluation.py`:

| arm | sens | spec | referral rate | PPV | referrals per true positive |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `lr_target_sensitivity` | 0.9231 | 0.1786 | 0.8224 | 0.011 | 89.1 |
| `lr_youden` | 0.7308 | 0.8214 | 0.1841 | 0.040 | 25.2 |
| `geopref_published` | 0.17 | 0.98 | 0.0215 | 0.079 | 12.6 |
| `gate_c_target` | 0.878 | 0.808 | 0.1989 | 0.044 | 22.6 |

The logistic-regression arms read `model.json`, so they cannot drift from it. This
table is the strongest pitch artifact produced this session: it shows in one row why
a rule-in instrument is the right shape for a Posyandu queue.

### The public validation page leads with accuracy, not agreement (Task 19)

The headline was 99.76% mean AOI agreement. The AOI boxes are 28% of screen width and
the median between-stream error is 4.1% of it, so that number was close to
arithmetically unavoidable. The page now leads with **2.36° median error against
held-out known targets** (94 Gate A sessions, p90 3.58°), drawn on a shared scale
beside WebGazer's own published 4.17°. Agreement is demoted, quoted at the recomputed
value, and carries the reason it saturates. ICC is reported as-is with the
variance-ratio caveat and Bland-Altman limits beside it, widest feature included.

`gate-b-public.json` is generated by `research/export_public_evidence.py` from the
canonical summaries. Edit the generator, never the JSON. `gate_a_summary.json` gained
a `knownTargetValidation` block so the accuracy claim has a canonical source.
